Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

TapBolt is a mobile tapping game. This policy explains what information the current version of TapBolt handles.

Information We Collect

The current version of TapBolt does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, or files. Game progress such as coins, scores, streaks and selected skins is stored locally on your device.

Internet and Third Parties

The current game build does not require an internet connection for core gameplay. If a future version adds advertising, analytics, online accounts or other third-party services, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated before those features are used.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information from the current version because the current version does not require collection of personal information.

Data Deletion

You can remove locally stored game data by clearing TapBolt app data or uninstalling the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact information provided on the official Google Play listing when TapBolt is published.
